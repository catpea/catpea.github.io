Multi-Agent For Young Ladies

11/2/2026

Multi Agent is where we are today, right this moment, and it is old news.

You can't just ask your AI to do something, humans have states of mind, AI has agents.

And just like you, agents prefer a clear state of mind.

For local AI that means less system memory, and less confusion.

You set the SOUL and SKILLs of the agent, you ask a question, get a response, and get out.

Name the group of skills after the job your agent performs, even if you have those skills evolve with the job.

Create quality assurance, user interface design, artists, philosophers, lawyers, and beta testers.

Have the SOUL dictate the way they work, and SKILL how a task is to be completed.

You dont need multiple AIs for this. you jut need one to wear different hats.

Message passing between multiple agents or personalities, can be as simple as exchanging text files, that reference their locations.

A simple process that has been proven to work is, plan, ask for 2 ideas, solve, test, review, repair and verify.

This is a bare bone process, that uses 5 agents, it has likely been optimized by AI and tested to deliver best.

I personally recommend that instead of having a plan agent, you use a human name, Elizabeth, Lilith, Eleanore.

And anthropomorphize the agent, to create an anchor in your memory.

In the future as you master it all, you will let go of names, and have AI arrange optimal networks of unnamed specialists.

plan ──┬── ideate-a ──┬── solve ── test ── review ── repair ── verify
       └── ideate-b ──┘

While you learn to create agents, use real names to enhance your and their abilities.

Discovering thinking machines, may just be the only break we will get.

For a hundred years, we will not be able to find something more precious.

It is like the internal combustion engine, or the boilers of the nuclear plant.

There will be no more, but what we improve and add around it.

Whether the AI of today is all we get, or we find something better and cheaper.

Multi-agent frameworks and, multi-agent workflows, will take it to the next level.

You must practice, model entire skyscrapers, model offices, model C-Suite CEOs and CTOs.

Before you know it, you will be able to run super fast and smart AI locally at home.

And what you learn from multi agent networks, will make it even more powerful, and even better at what it does.
