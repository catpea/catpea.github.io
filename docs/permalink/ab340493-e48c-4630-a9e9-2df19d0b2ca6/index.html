Software Deployment For Young Ladies

Programming November 23, 2026 13:51 of narration 4 min read

Listen · 13:51

MP3

So, I used smart AI to create an integrated development environment for local AI,
so that it has an easy time using it to create products, this time web themes.

I have a powerful web theme generator,
that creates $49 products, for free, while I sleep.

I don’t even ship images, just beautiful generative art, think screensavers,
a single well compressed product is less than a megabyte.

But, having a product, often means, uploading to somebody else’s store,
and them taking a cut, it is fun to see how that works, but is it needed?

And I can assure you, they will check your website, your other products,
because you can’t show them one product.

You have to show them that it is one,
of many.

In your case it should be games, starting from pong, asteroids, and galaga,
onto marble madness, bobble bobble and rise of the triad.

Don’t ask your AI for a specific game though, ask it for a good game,
that makes good use of Phaser, Pixi.js, or [Three.js] even.

This is how you hit the random button,
make it a classic, choose from the underdogs, the underappreciated.

In my case, I may end up being my own customer,
maybe my only customer.

I may take the products I created,
and make them even more powerful and expensive targeting specific programs.

WordPress, Astro, Ghost, Hexo, Hugo, Jekyll,
and whatever else I forgot.

There my web theme becomes easily installable,
and a click of a button refreshes someones entire website.

But we can be certain that an upload is necessary,
and you will need to process credit cars.

I can think of few companes, cloudflare, vercel, render for hosting,
and paypal and stripe for credit card processing.

You may sell stuff inside games, and your games may be sold,
by the maker of your phone’s operating system.

Unless you publish on the web,
maybe using NativeScript and its canvas.

If you are going to upload a product,
it can’t be to a blog, you will need to ask your AI for a lightweight marketplace.

I decided to built pea-shop, and I split it in two parts,
the actual store that just does what stores are meant to do.

And then a pea command an control center,
which is where the commands to control the store live.

There are o administrative utilities that I ship to the web,
the store does not even have a start command.

I told my AI, to simulate cloudflare,
and that comes out of the command and control center, not any server.

And my products are just directories with zip files,
you don’t even need a command to manage them.

Though I did add them, but also told my AI to write a tutorial,
it is still very much directory based.

My theme builder injects products to my control center,
and then a command can push them to the internet.

Don’t be afraid of the technologies,
that are underneath the system, just tell your AI to make it secure.

It will do a better job than any programmer,
or even white hat pen-terster.

It used to be fun to learn about FTP, sFFTP, telnet, ssh,
but today the services are called a letter and a number.

And each company has their own version,
their own idea of what it means to upload a file or store a record.

You ask AI about domain name registration, I use porkbun,
I highly recommend you choose a funny name, I still giggle at catpea.

And you keep asking, is this secure, how would even hackers break in,
and you sure as sunshine can add logging to tell you of suspicious activity.

But all websites everywhere are always being scanned,
in just the last few days PHP, Java, and Ruby had remote code execution bugs.

AI will keep you more secure, by writing lightweight software,
then if you were using a large program, that everyone used for the last 25 years.

And I wrote all my software in the supremely lightweight JavaScript,
everyone supports it, including hosting companies and browsers.

You create products by making an IDE for your local AI,
and letting it randomize product structure, make a random template.

And then again, you ask your AI, to create a web store,
and inject products into it, maybe hundreds of computer puzzles and games.

Tell it to walk across the history,
and remake the classics.

Then almost as a second thought you make a store, set a price,
and take care of advertising.

Like with skating, your first few products will be crappy,
but that can only improve, because you have something to stand on now.

Don’t worry about security, or the technology beneath,
just ask your AI to make it secure, and lightweight, and ship it.
