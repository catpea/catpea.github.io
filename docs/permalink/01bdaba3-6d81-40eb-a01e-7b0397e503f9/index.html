How To Really Talk To Your AI - A Message For Young Ladies

9/9/2026

The first and foremost is the creation of a platform, A platform is like a game engine without the game.

You have all the tools you need to create a game, But you only have Mario as an example game.

Then you ask your AI to build the tools it needs, To operate the platform towards your aims.

I recommend you deal with music at first, You just fell files that never need upgrades.

And your songs can be about anything, Including the re-telling of some long book.

Or even teaching advanced programming concepts, Or making philosophers a household name.

Think of it as Mario, You are just learning.

Personally, I created an experimental platform, Based on text adventure worlds.

Because AI talks text, And text adventure games understand text.

And I say such things as, "What is our platform unique good at?"

Or, I say to the AI, "I have a investor meeting", "Let us put together some daring new features."

I just now told my AI, that, "I am mining for new advanced functionality".

And I explained, that I am seeking something, That is few milestones away.

My text adventure platform has no aim, It is a pure research platform.

So I keep pushing AI, To invent new ends.

In your music platform, You should ask for multiple business models.

You want one website, That offers an iTunes like download.

Where you can sell songs for $1.99, Using a processor like PayPal or stripe.

Another website where people need to subscribe, For free downloads and later ad removal.

Another platform, that lets the customer, Describe the song, and your AI makes lyrics.

Then you want a phone app, And specialized apps like gym songs.

Gym songs are divided into weeks, And in the first weeks songs are slower.

You should probably have a speaker, That reminds the user to drink water.

To aim to eliminate rest, And slowly move onto faster songs.

The amazing thing about growing up in the age of AI.

Is that you are not supposed to, Do all the programing, because there is no time.

You will have many businesses to monitor, And they must all have an AI brain.

In closing, I need you to ignore, The anti-AI people.

The sloppers, only call AI as ugly, Until they can make a profit - they are not friends.

But to use music as example, Your songs need a theme.

The same way that artists, Have persons, sometimes multiple.

You choose whatever moves you, And that gives you a great advantage.

As that is the path, That you will beat your competition on.
