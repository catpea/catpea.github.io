Coding Agents Are A Silly Idea, and Programmers Are Silly Too

Programming June 11, 2026 15:50 of narration 2 min read

Listen · 15:50

MP3

You know the cheerful old folks,
who win the lottery and still live the way they always lived.

Then to donate their winning to an institution,
that has destroyed generations of students with standardized education.

That is what programmers are doing today, they won the lottery,
the AI is all about programming, and nothing else.

The new models are the old AIs in a conventional harness,
that multiples their abilities to no end.

Wait, wait, there is more, programming it self, is, and has forever been,
all about artificial intelligence.

If, then, else if, else, is artificial intelligence,
every programmer has always worked on AI.

But, due to fake education, and bad operating systems and languages,
being spoonfed at college – programmers think poorly.

To quote a wise man: “You can take the programmer out of the river of tears,
but you can’t take their van in which they live in, down by that same river.”

A coding agent must be understood as an oxymoron,
the coding has to be finished already.

Via a large process library that helps small local agents,
not do more of the same stuff we did before AI.

But to model advanced Desktop Applications,
based on strict procedures in its library.

Wait, wait, wait, the first two applications it models,
are:

A rich, and thick, and detailed, and precise, and lovely, and informative,
Process Library browser, think of it as a small-agent-brain extension manager.

One of the major components of that library are meta processes,
that help smaller agents call the big AI and extend the brain.

And the second application, is a drag and drop user interface builder,
it uses AI to generate layouts, starting templates, and input forms and such.

But the user can just drag and drop out of the 500+ library,
of stored complex, well made, user interfaces components.

Do you see any coding agents here, no,
maintenance personnel, and an application building assistant.

You will need a coding agent to create this,
but then you get rid of the ladder, coding agents are not meant for daily use.

Then what?
you now have the ability to create incredibly intricate applications.

The components and their documentation alone are enough for big AI
to construct the Event Sourcing backed, tried and tested, don’t mess with it.

You get to come home, and build purpose specific applications,
on the level of top selling desktop applications, and you can sell them too.

Personally, I recommend you keep building higher,
remember applications are a form of artificial intelligence.

Put three advanced applications together,
to create the forth one.

You could build a real University,
one that actually teaches for real.

Here students are actually allowed to grow their genius,
not by considerable adversity...

But joy of life, love of learning and exploration,
that original quest, for greatness.
