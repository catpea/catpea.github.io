The New Hope: Programming For Ladies

8/13/2026

Look at a computer application, any application will do.

Look how things are nested, window, form, text inputs, buttons.

Up until few months ago, maybe days, this required a human to arrange, to nest.

And sticking human fingers here, mean that it was humans from now on.

This means every computer application, is worthless.

Big corporate software, is beyond worthless.

It needs the finger of hundreds, maybe thousands of humans.

It is a really nasty situation, constant manual labor.

Now imagine the nested appliciaon before you, starting to sink into the monitor.

The window is on the surface, the side bar or form is deeper.

The labels, input boxes, deeper still.

The nesting is revealing it self, as depth that represents context and complexity.

This depth, is where you unleash AI, you get it down into an input box.

And say, make input boxes smarter, give them graphic styles and rounded edges.

The AI will dig in deeper still, upgrading repairing, focused.

Recusing into the microscopic areas, that no human finger can touch.

That would take thousands of programmers, or hundreds of years of programming.

We made it into the age of artificial intelligence, and we have some cleaning up to do.

We don't want to throw away programmers, but we need to stop them from programming by hand.

And that means, you don't need to program by hand, to work on state of the art programming AI.

It is better, that you were not exposed to conventional programming.

Because conventional programming, is now legacy.

It makes programmers micromanage machines, that are clearly smarter than them.

And that is a pretty dumb thing to do, and that is why we need you.

You are the generation that begins with AI, that is the post legacy generation.

The better, generation, the better programmer.

DO not listen to critics, at the end of the day.

When their minds become slow, and fingers too fat.

They ask AI for a solution, they end up asking the questions you do.

This is not about the new fad of AI recursing, this is about focusing your AI, a wise practice.

But also about making updates, to reusable components, not applicaions.

So that when AI does make an upgrade, that automatically enhances all your other programs.

You have the depth of vision, and universal upgrades.

And all you need, is an AI that understands your vision.

That is all you, that is your programming and your imagination.

And because you are not weighted down by legacy, and won't micromanage your systems will be more advanced.

You may get stuck at first, but then you ask you AI:

What can we do to refactor, to upgrade.

To use the existing system as wisdom, for your new hope.
