Launch Days For Young Ladies

Today, you don’t ask, if you have a product,
you ask to create a product, and then make it better to rise above others.

Before you know it, your best customers will be the AI/s,
to them your product represents time and energy saved.

I chose web design,
web themes to be precise.

But I want you to choose web computer games,
and node.js game servers that make them go.

This way you have a complete product,
my web themes are not a complete product.

But mu finish line is dictated by my customers,
they want to be the ones finishing.

Your customers, will want to build on-line entertainment centers,
and you need to provide them with fun and customizable games.

My AI spent the entire day on about 10 large features,
typography, bottleneck elimination, hot server upgrades.

It resent many of my second generation artwork,
for a third, more informed regenration.

An it installed qwen-27b heavily quantized that runs at 70 tokens,
my gpt-oss-20b, is running at, 170t/s must faster, but not as smart.

Before I walked out of my house I requested, a model router,
so my AI will route coding requests based on intelligence and price.

Your smartest AI should only do the planning, the smaller do the coding,
remember, the smart AIs can do black hole math, don’t use them for coding.

And always ask your main coding AI to efficiently use its own agents,
remember the smart-ones should only be planning, save your usage.

I recommend you make 10 games, and only select 5 as first wave,
and then once you get a feel for it, work on one game at a time.

I have 50 themes,
but only 10 for sale.

40 of my themes are to be given away free,
under the agreement that they link back to my store.

In time, I will continue developing the 40,
but under a Pro version.

When your games start confusing AI,
stop asling the AI for creating games directly.

Ask it to create a friendly but AI operated,
game development studio, each type of game gets its own studio.

My web themes are just one type of product,
application themes, that use my web themes, are done in a different program.

There I start a new project and provide the web theme,
and AI the AI makes a product worth several times more.

You might have installed a wordpress or a CMS theme before,
web themes are about design, application themes provide pages like checkout.

You launch through automation,
you do not fill out forms that can be automated.

You need the ability to erase a project,
and re-upload, its perfect version.

This give you the ability to have multiple companies,
what you can do once, you should do many times.

And finally, there is no such thing as slop, that is a word made up by elitists,
and those who want to turn your family into wage slaves.

Poverty is a form of abuse, it boils you slowly like a frog,
AI gives you the strengths, to get out of the slaughterhouse.

Lastly, make a game that teaches vim and i3 shortcuts,
instead of jumping right thee times make the user say 3w.

But even a better idea is to start in the beginning,
asteroids, galaga, toki, and upgrade the idea into your own direcion.

Wireframe space oddysey, AI can program 3D, pixelart conquest game,
where space invaders are launded to conquer the milky way.

And a pixel art nature world, where you don’t really fight anything,
and if you stop waking around the sounds of nature surround you.

Make something very old, very new again, to inherit its wisdom.
and always ask the player to plugin their AI to power your game characters.

Give the characters life,
and give them wisdom.
