Is A Strict Dress Code Policy Keeping You From Celebrating Halloween?

Society September 18, 2026 19:22 of narration 7 min read

Listen · 19:22

MP3

Halloween is certainly the only holiday worth celebrating,
aside from all the candy it is a cosmic celebration.

Many workplaces will allow you to wear religious symbols,
that often represent hundreds of millions of lost lives.

Very few allow you to celebrate the bounty of nature,
the passage of season, and the fact that we each still have a tail bone.

All the so called facts aside, we are an animal and in broader sense,
and earth organism, genetically not all that dissimilar from a little tomato.

Despite all the imaginary boarders, we all share ancestors,
we are all relatives.

Tricked to believe that we are born poor and thus must work.
overzealous companies optimize their profits by telling us how to dress.

What not to wear, how to think, what is right,
and what is optimal for your performance.

Until you stage a proper uprising, I recommend make up,
each day, a little bit heavier.

You may wish to start early in the year,
but even so, before you know you will be sent to HR.

Where an older woman, will be itching, if not down right dying,
to speak the following spell: “Your make up is too heavy.”

To which you must slowly reply “You are too heavy”,
and appreciate that you have reached a new level of existence.

You have just taken the HEAVY responsibility,
for your own appearance.

This is not a joke, most people, are born, live, and die,
without making a peep.

On your fist day after the HR meeting,
you must paint over the bride of your nose.

You have just begun your journey as a warrior,
it is unlikely HR will see you before you walk off onto the Appalachian Trail.

You can see how revealing that is,
you step onto a cradle of yourself.

Where the white blaze becomes your crucible,
and by the time you return from the Pacific Crest and Continental Divide.

You will see that your Raccoon Make up,
was live or die, obesity or conquest, six feet under, or the stars.

What you have been made to forget,
is that each of your ancestors was a warrior, a powerful triumphant warrior.

Halloween candy, is a cover for one of your last of your chances,
to return to the Quest, the walking trail that actually does make a difference.

All together the Appalachian, Pacific Crest, and Continental Divide Trails,
are known as the Triple Crown of hiking.

The animal you wear, in a complexity most humans never grasp,
becomes your trail name.

Each human being is meant to undertake a great quest,
so that their mind can develop beyond polite servitude.

You won’t know what you animal is until your end,
but you can be sure it is not polite, meek, or scared.

It is a fierce force of nature, inexhaustible,
untamed, and when an occasion call for it feral and rabid.

You are not poor, you are not a servant,
culture has nothing to teach you.

You will discover decency, dignity, courage,
nobility, authenticity and greatness, on your Great Quest.

The only, holy thing,
that has ever existed.

Gentle, nearly invisible, black liner,
is your first step onto the trail.

Don't stop, grow all the way up,
until you become a great being.
