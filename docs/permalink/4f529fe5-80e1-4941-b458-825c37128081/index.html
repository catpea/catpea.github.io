Intro To Software Publishing For Young Ladies

11/13/2026

AI has improved this week, again, the leading model appears to be rather flawless.

I made an Operating System, Visual Programming Language, a Pixel Art Interval Timer, and finished the Pro Version of Nine Slice.

The details of my first computer programs are not as interesting, as the fact that they are near completion, and maybe complete.

I specifically told the AI, that I need it to finish, and I told it to use NativeScript and Canvas targeting every platform.

I asked a free AI what sells, and it said Mobile & Business Software.

Mobile because it is in your customers pocket, and business software because it makes them money.

Throwing advanced applications at your customers, will only scare them away, and to fix this, put them on a web desktop.

Sell an operating system with business applications, or a desktop with amazing games, not one thing, not complicated first.

And it must be software that runs on small screens, and you need to be able to say: oh by the way, here is a complete web demo.

When you request a finished program from AI, it must also automatically create the final files your customer pays for.

AI must take care of your needs, every step of the way.

It must also set up a publishing pipeline that lands your applications in app stores as far as that is possible.

You should receive an email that the application is under review, and your inbox must be managed by an AI agent.

Support, advertising, showing your product to real users, is all managed or done by AI.

The best part isn’t automation, but that your products must be lovely.

Just generating an app is easy, anybody can do it now.

You have to great good applications, and like all good art, they must be things that change lives.

Human robots will tell you to find a niche market, but that is not how it is done.

You have to find within you what is personally interesting, and make it personal and valuable to yourself.

Today, all the best selling apps are impersonal, blurred, they target lowest common denominators.

But when you create create applications in a style that you like, with features that you wish were always there, that is a winner.

For example, a distraction free word processor, with a powerful timeline, and work divided into a tree.

Pages where you can add stickers, pushpins, floating sticky notes, and mind maps.

You see it it the harmonious combination of features that you personally want to use, that makes a program useful, to some, not all.

You can now sit down design your application on paper, and then describe it to the AI, mentioning that you want it complete.

You want to have proper actions that upload to websites or stores, that feature or sell your product.

Orchestration as I have described above, is just programming.

You use English, not a programming language, and that was always the promise, high level programming languages.

Come up with a program that you would use, ask for completion, CI/CD, and proper actions.

And than use the AI that your code repositories come with, and either connect and agent to your email, of use the AI that your email has.

The biggest challenge here is coming up with interesting application ideas, thing about what is fun about computers, what apps and games you like.

And then, improve on those ideas.
