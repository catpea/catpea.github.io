Online Empires For Young Ladies

Programming October 25, 2026 8:55 of narration 3 min read

Listen · 8:55

MP3

You must use linux as your platform,
its authentication and home directories for user data.

The college gran in their fake-education incompetence,
creates a custom user management system - you are now wiser than that.

My AI and I chose a custom node.js solution,
based on wisdom of the Cockpit project and related solutions.

But don't jump into custom solutions like I did,
I do it as part of my research and education.

Talk to your AI about Cockpit,
Virtualmin + Usermin and others.

Candidate Where it is strongest Fit for your idea
Virtualmin + Usermin Managing a business built around Linux hosting accounts Strongest starting point for account provisioning, domains, quotas, and customer administration
JupyterHub + SystemdSpawner Starting and managing a separate web application for each Linux user The most interesting challenger for running your own desktop application
Open OnDemand Giving Linux users a substantial browser application portal Especially compelling as customers gain more applications and computing capabilities
Ajenti Building an extensible panel with authenticated Unix-user sessions A genuine Cockpit alternative, with a different development framework
Cockpit Connecting browser JavaScript directly to the logged-in user’s Linux environment Particularly attractive when minimizing custom integration code

What is important here is that you use linux,
as a complexity and a security shield.

Custom code is often confusing,
but AI is really god at setting up linux.

Give the first server you set-up a name and personality,
that you can share with your customers.

Call it the Sol Invidious server,
the Unconquered Sun.

Don't hide the server, allow yoursef to say,
we are upgrading the Unconquered Sun this friday.

All the users will be slowly placed on Moon Moon Server,
starting AI will ensure no service interruptions will occur.

Let your customers understand what you system status page means,
let them enjoy the triumph of a perfect upgrade.

And always treat therm to an upgraded UI,
they have to see the new.

I repeat, with each upgrade they get a more beautiful UI,
amaze.

Do not ask your AI what what it can do with linux,
ask what linux can do for you.

Do not twist linux,
use its power in full.

Setup your server for a couple of dollars as soon as possibe,
be your own customer, enjoy the dreams.

Finally, and you better sit down for this one,
let me tell you what you put on the server for each of your users.

You give them a web based desktop,
with a app store - some apps pre-installed.

So they get a linux account on your server,
and when they login, a web desktop with an app.

What app is up to you,
but allow me to suggest three.js based appliciaons.

They look like wild sci-fi futuristic user interfaces,
and they can show or manage complex things.

Your AI can send in your private project reports,
for visualization on a multiverse timeline.

Or you can drag and drop projects,
that give AI strong instructions.

Everything mostly runs in the browser,
data is on server, customer is reminded of a backup copy.

Give your customers luxury apps,
that help them make money, or room in their head.

Your empire is a web operating system,
with its own app store.

You can chage at signup, per credit,
per app, use shareware for your web apps, or freemium.

This only takes a couple of days to make,
though your app store may only have notepad.

And perhaps neat there.js hacking game,
that costs a dollar to load into the account.

Please remember, linux, web-desktop, app-store,
notepad, and some game for a $1.

AI knows every step of this,
it is just regular programming with an extra step.

You see this way you build your empire,
before your killer apps is ready.

You have a sturdy target, clear aim,
and your app store waiting for your app.
