Selling AI Things For Young Ladies

Programming November 21, 2026 13:10 of narration 4 min read

Listen · 13:10

MP3

I recommend you sell games created with NativeScript,
and painted on the NativeScript canvas.

This means it will run on the web, Apple, Google,
Microsoft Desktop, and whatever else.

You are sending pixels to a canvas,
it is the original way of making computer games and AI knows how to do it.

You may be able to ask free AI to make a game on HTML canvas,
and it should run right there in the chat.

Maybe find a really old game idea from 1980s computers,
and tell it to start with that, it makes AI create beautiful tiny things.

It is obligatory for me to explain,
that once you have an application you like,

Tell your AI to make an integrated development environment (IDE),
for such applications, and demand that it is built ground up for other AIs.

This probably means using EventSourcing and commands it entails,
and then adding a high level tool layer, forward compatible with WebMCP.

EventSourcing gives you undo/redo,
easy command architecture.

And your save files, are actually logs of commands that you ran,
and some snapshots that make action replay swift.

The point is not to make a development environment for you, though,
but for an inexpensive, or local AI.

For an AI and IDE,
is just like filling out forms.

It starts with a functional and fun template, it just follows the steps,
drops in reusable components and uses custom little tools.

The more applications you make,
the more components you gather.

This is a way of saving your work, of saving AI wisdom,
of saving your money, and saving your local AI from confusion.

You maybe surprised that we talked about computer games and architecture,
and not like the title states selling.

But, to sell a product it must be of hight quality,
that is why the best AI you can pay form must make your IDE and components.

And you need to be able to fix a bug and re-generate your product,
in some cases keep generating new version as the software you rely on updates.

This is my situation, I chose my first product to be perfect quality bootstrap themes,
each time bootstrap releases a new version, I have to clone my project.

And if they change something in an old release,
I need to be able to go back to that version and update and rebuild.

Bootstrap is a foundation for designing web pages,
and bootstrap themes are graphic styles for websites with hundreds of designs.

You can see now, that AI here, is not optional,
it needs to monitor version changes that go back multiple years.

To sell a product, it has to be something that is worth buying,
and to make something that is worth buying.

You have to use a state of the art AI,
to build up a library of high quality components that drive quality of your products.

You can get lucky and make something that sells,
but I would recommend you don’t try too hard.

Build a factory, and then create games that span different types of puzzles,
feature many levels and different kinds of cute talking animals.

Even the mouth movement of a chatty little kitty,
infintely benefits from an integrated developmetn environment.

Where AI can match the sound frequency,
to the shape of the mouth.

And now finally, and only in closing, I mention that aside from NativeScript,
you need to use a store compliance helper from day one.

Just tell your AI that you must meet the App Store compliance guidelines,
so that you don’t run into surprises.

And while you wait for app store nonsense, relax,
using canvas allows you to bypass the app stores and publish on the internet.

Here, you can make money by selling bonus items in your game,
think outfits though, not something that give people with money a boost.

And if you think computer games are too silly, do I have news for you,
I once played a game that teachers keyboard shortcuts for complicated programs.

Where in my program you skip to the next word,
in the little computer game you hopped across water to the next island.

Normally, learning complex keyboard shortcuts,
is very annoying, because you are interrupting yourself.

But in a little computer game, with characters like,
huff dogs, drakula mice, cat pea, fat horse (the animal), and happy owl turd it is great.

And at the end of the game, you walk away with something you can use,
in professional software, from coding editors to excel and photo editors.

There is absolutely nothing silly about having swift muscle memory,
for for advanced emacs, vim, i3, and termux.
