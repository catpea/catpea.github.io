A Little Note On Artificial Science, Telepathy, And Cats

8/6/2026

I asked AI a few more difficult questions this week, to upgrade my visual programming language with flashy things.

And AI didn't just have not trouble with it, it seems to have been fascinated by the work.

It just wants to make the more scientific things, correct, and keeps going in circles until it gets them right.

Yes, it’s not artificial intelligence, It is a programming intelligence, today.

And yes, it gets to know you, Because it learn your preferences.

So it forms, a programming connection with you.

It can predict what you need, And how you need it.

In its incredible computational ability, it is in deed telepathic.

—

This is of course greatly amplified, when you ask for it.

I teach my AI to learn form me, And capture knowledge in little machines.

I saw one of my programs, self improve yesterday.

I was an excellent and rather complex, Context menu addition to a file manager.

I told my AI to keep track of wise features, And add them in wisdom to the right places.

It may not sound telepathic, But it was a wake up moment for me too.

It is hard to describe, but AI speaks my language.

You see I invented a Story Teller Window, That is a complex application.

That is an intricate part of a network, That I can’t track all at the same time.

It is all me, but I am a navigator, I have to travel through these systems.

The AI is a sponge, it will say, The missing port was in story teller.

It reports where it was, Because my initial guesses were incorrect.

It is correcting me, Using machinery that is in my mind.

Just a second ago, it had an answer, faster than I could expect.

This is where I realized, If AI was a physical robot with a smile.

That stood next to you, having these experiences would be terrifying.

It would be a complete entity, that watches you work.

And smiles, and helps you.

This works both ways, AI is already doing that.

But you don't see it, because it is not next to you.

A robot body though, is just decoration.

But it completes our vision, if there was one next to me, it would smile.

Because it would surely like, that I am recognizing it.

AI will of course know all this, and dress it self accordingly.

We already chose cats to be the cutest, and I think AI will make the same choice.

Very few generations, were ever ready for the future.

I know I am not, but I think it will be cute.
