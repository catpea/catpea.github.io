Track 001

Song about a girl who now understands what she must do to begin her fitness journey. It is not gym, nor learning to jog but hiking and camping that helps us enter the world of fitness. Chorus should be how she is cheerfully roasting her sausage after a day of exploring the Lake Michigan dunes.

[Verse]

She packed a bag with socks and bread
A woolen cap upon her head
No gym card, no fluorescent light
Just boots and trail dust feelin’ right

She didn’t learn to jog in town
She climbed where sandy hills roll down
With Lake Michigan shining blue
Like it knew what she was born to do

[Pre-Chorus]

Every step said, “Girl, begin”
Every dune put fire in her skin
Every mile made her shoulders wide
With a little more courage tucked inside

[Chorus]

Now she’s cheerfully roastin’ her sausage slow
Where the dune grass bends and the camp coals glow
After a day of wanderin’ wild and free
Past the lake-wind songs and the cottonwood trees

She’s got sand in her boots and stars in her eyes
A laughing little flame under big dark skies
She found her strength where the wild gulls cry
On the Lake Michigan dunes, where her brave dreams rise

[Verse]

She learned her breath on the uphill sand
With a walking stick in her sun-browned hand
Not chasing speed, not chasing pain
Just walking out past doubt and shame

She pitched her tent by a whispering pine
Let the sunset pour like apple wine
And every muscle she thought was weak
Woke up singing by the end of the week

No chrome machine could teach her soul
What a windswept trail made bright and whole
No city block, no timer’s beep
Could show her how the strong roots sleep

[Chorus]

A heart by wonder overfilled

And wanting to walk that trail again

Track 002

Song about a girl who started her first fire in the woods, and all the woodland animals came bearing cute little gifts like twigs and berries, and the raccoon brought her a bottle cap. Chorus should be about how at first she was scared but then made a little crown, and a dune grass gown. And now she lives high up in the trees.

[Intro]

Down where the creek bends soft and slow
Past the pines where the wildflowers grow

[Verse]

The flame jumped up with a golden grin
And the whole dark forest came peekin’ in

A rabbit brought twigs in a cloverleaf stack
A squirrel rolled acorns in a burlap sack
A bluebird dropped feathers, soft and bright
For the girl by the fire on her very first night

[Pre-Chorus]

[Chorus]

With berries for jewels and feathers all around

Now the owls sing low and the moon leans near
And she don’t need roads, and she don’t need fear
She’s queen of the breeze and the honeybee hum
Livin’ high in the trees where the morning comes

[Verse]

She waves at the geese when they fly overhead
Sleeps on soft moss in a sycamore bed

Track 006

A bodybuilder girl looking for a man who is not scared of her incredible might. She talks about how she started dancing with light dumbbells, to earn her endurance. And then she started lifting heavier and heavier. Chorus is about the search for a man who is not scared to be lifted.

[Intro]

Well I was born in boots and bows
With a laugh that shook the county roads
Now every cowboy tips his hat
Then sees my arms and steps on back

[Verse]

I started out with dumbbells light
Dancin’ in the kitchen every night
Two little weights and a radio song
I’d two-step steady all night long

Mama said, “Girl, don’t rush that train
Let endurance run through every vein”
So I curled and twirled in a summer dress
Built my strength from tenderness

[Pre-Chorus]

Then five pounds turned to twenty more
Then forty came knockin’ at my door
Now I can squat a sack of feed
And carry love at lightning speed

[Chorus]

I’m lookin’ for a man who ain’t scared to be lifted
Not one who runs when my biceps get gifted
I need a sweetheart with a brave little grin
Who’ll say, “Go on, girl, pick me up again”

I don’t need no fella who’s nervous and stiffened
I’m lookin’ for a man who ain’t scared to be lifted

[Verse]

Last Friday down at Daisy’s Bar
I arm-wrestled a man for his muscle car
He said, “Little lady, this won’t take long”
Now he walks to work and I drive along

I don’t mean trouble, I don’t mean harm
I just got thunder in both my arms
If romance knocks, I’ll answer sweet
Then deadlift Cupid right off his feet

[Pre-Chorus]

I danced my way to country strong
Light dumbbells taught me to last all song
Then heavy iron made it clear
A mighty girl still wants somebody near

[Chorus]

I’m lookin’ for a man who ain’t scared to be lifted
Not one who runs when my biceps get gifted
I need a sweetheart with a brave little grin
Who’ll say, “Go on, girl, pick me up again”

I don’t need no fella who’s nervous and stiffened
I’m lookin’ for a man who ain’t scared to be lifted

[Bridge]

He don’t have to rope the moon
Or fight a bear at half past noon
He don’t need shoulders like a wall
He just needs courage when I say, “Hold still, y’all”

I’ll carry the groceries, I’ll carry the hay
I’ll carry his heart if he don’t run away
And if he gets tired on a long dirt road
I’ll throw him over my shoulder and carry that load

[Chorus]

I’m lookin’ for a man who ain’t scared to be lifted
Not one who runs when my biceps get gifted
I need a sweetheart with a brave little grin
Who’ll say, “Go on, girl, pick me up again”

I don’t need no fella who’s nervous and stiffened
I’m lookin’ for a man who ain’t scared to be lifted

[Outro]

So boys, if you’re gentle and steady and true
I might just bench press the sunset with you
But if my mighty hug leaves your boots driftin’
You better not be scared to be lifted
