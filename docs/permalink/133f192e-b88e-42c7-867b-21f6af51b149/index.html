A Dangerous Turning Point: Cutting Mats For Cutting Shapes

Fitness November 24, 2026 8:14 of narration 4 min read

Listen · 8:14

MP3

Many years ago, two enterprising lads in Paradip,
came into possession of a large spool of high grade, high density poly ethylene.

It was cheap, passed down through bankruptcies, buyouts, scams and auctions,
they only know they needed, because of its size and low price.

What they needed it for, was unclear,
just like this whole story, is unclear.

It sat in the yard for the entertainment of the chickens,
who turned pooing on it, in an art form.

When time came to get rid of it, one of the gentlemen, could not cut it,
and so an idea was born.

To the consternation of three generation of chickens,
it would be sold, as a vegetable cutting mat.

For Americans, who like vegetables,
and useless crap.

Many years after, and an entire fortune later,
the little old waddled into a dollar store, looking for trouble.

My local gym, has rubber coated floors,
I have rubber coated shoe.

And my ankles have a problem with that,
it is not possible to workout in a gym with rubber floors.

Because a workout requires a dance,
and a dance demand that your shoes so not stick to the floor.

Originally, I tried a for sale sign, considering,
that I only need some plastic, I tried a placement mat with Gwen Stacy on it.

I tried hard plastic, meant for moving a couch on a carpet,
and interesting looking thick felt creations meant for wooden floors.

But, something was wrong, I asked an AI, and it said,
increase friction coefficient – so I figured vegetable cutting mats.

I found, what turned out to be,
the last batch of the chicken poop vegetable mats.

Using carpet tape, I stuck the HDPE to the bottom of my shoe,
and transformed by body, with a hundred new muscles.

Including, the re-awaking of my tail muscles,
but that is a story for Halloween.

For a couple of years, I would go back and get more, and stick more on my shoe,
a soleful lasts about three weeks.

Six months ago, I bought a batch, and discovered,
that it was a different type of plastic more polycarbonate like.

And though it was better than rubber,
my angles would start hurting within the hour.

Store after store, I could not find any of the last chicken batch,
today, I found the last 30 sheets, and bought them all for $20 dollars.

I don’t know the exact chemistry,
and certainly the product label does speak the truth.

But my best guess is that it is Polyethylene and that the replacement,
is Polycarbonate, and recently I also tested a Polypropylene cutting board.

Both the Polycarbonate and Polypropylene (which was listed on the product),
did not glide on a chemically compromised recycled tire rubber floor.

I just barely managed to have a backup, I had a pair of templates,
that I used two weeks two weeks ago, and too care of them really well.

It you can pretty much see through it, or if it is labeled as polypropylene,
it will hurt your feet.

And you have to use vegetable cutting mats,
not the thicker cutting boards as they would be tot rough on the floor.

The chicken favorite, or what I guess is the high grade HDPE,
must be a millimeter thin, three millimeters maybe already too much.

You can’t have a workout with out dance,
the lie of proper posture has destroyed countless bodies and posture.

You have to glide, you have to gently come to a stop,
eventually you maybe dancing with 40 pounds of inertia packed dumbbells.

That moment can’t land at your mid sole, that is eventually painful,
it is the the connection between the sole and the floor that must absorb it.

Sure, you will crack your head on the ceramic tile in the locker room,
it you don’t watch out how you move.

Coming in contact with a short bristle HDPE carper,
as the gyms are fond or having, will have you fall.

The bristles in the carpet remove nearly all friction,
a high five will have you spin around five times - but the dancing it worth it.

I will sure run out by next winter or so, and if I can’t find a replacement,
then my ultimate solution is to bring a patch of some floor to my gym.

This is similar to how Yoga ladies have yoga mats,
I will just bring a dance sized square of something that is more slidy.
