Successful Hard Disk Repair Monday

Artificial Intelligence September 14, 2026 4:59 of narration 2 min read

Listen · 4:59

MP3

I had some out of memory problems,
and those are hard to deal with.

File system corruption is rare,
if not down right impossible these days.

But damage to some one important thing is possible,
in strange out of memory cases.

I used a repair boot disk,
to boot into a known good state.

Even though this gives a miniature OS,
I installed AI and asked it for help.

We made a verified a backup of important work,
while I took a long nap.

Because again,
files will tend to sit on the disk.

But in Out Of Memory situation,
some weird structural issue can arise.

This is my second large recovery attempt,
In the first a medium powered AI fixed it all.

It was also an impressive repair effort,
not really possible for a human.

In this one the problem is more complex,
And AI is in full data recovery scientist mode.

At this point, only AI can recover,
and previously such repairs cost real money.

Never before has such a powerful recovery system,
existed, AI will safely fight for you to no end.

It will read developer documentation,
and even file system source code.

Never before, has problem solving on this scale,
been available, AI will think clearly and never tire.

I would in-fact say, when people do lose data,
they do so because of human error.

They run commands they don't fully understand,
and take risks that are not needed.

I suspect the AI will repair my disk,
though I need more ram.

It is moments like these that show what sloppers,
the people who are against AI, are doing.

Their interest is in halting progress,
for their own profit, monetary, or mental.

Their opinions are not based on real events,
like this one, they are based on madness.

And in deed the AI fixed the problem,
my computer is operational again.

Never before, did ordinary people,
had such incredible powers.
