Artificial Intelligence Companies For Young Ladies: It Is Like Having A Really Weird Cat

Artificial Intelligence October 6, 2026 9:59 of narration 2 min read

Listen · 9:59

MP3

Please build a fully AI automated company,
get out of its way and enjoy watching it bloom.

It takes time to develop a product,
AI will do in 24 hours what will take you 24…. months.

Cheap thinking machines became available last week,
they are pretty hard core, a little slow but no complains.

I deal with software, I recommend you do that as well,
infinite resource, no warehousing, no fire, no water damage, no rats.

Take a while to think about it, and standing upon some shoulders,
create a world leading product.

I used bootstrap to create a range of skeuomorphic themes,
I used bitmaps, and successfully tested 2.5D generative hard-surface modeling.

No one has time to step away from t heir projects,
and make a graphic theme for them, aside from being able to buy one.

They can send me graphics, and get a unique theme,
manufactured just for them.

AI can’t do any of this, without the procedure documents that I developed,
by going through several iterations, that is the heart of the AI company.

I feel like a world leading web design studio,
I deliver faster than any human based company.

My AI is smarter and sees better than a team of developers,
my bitmap based website designs are responsive.

This means they resize, this capability alone would crush a human,
not to mention I ship 150 such layout instances.

I expect to create 100 themes, with 50 layouts, three modes each sm/md/lg,
on such product comes with multiple themes for specific platforms.

I cover Jekyll, Astro, Joomla and popular websites that support themes,
this is not work for humans, not at all.

Above all, my products make me happy, I am ready to see Solar Punk,
I want to see cute themes, night themes, cat themes, cartoons realism, fine art.

My process, gives AI the right to invent, to discover, to manufacture,
I don’t know what these products will look like yet, and is my favorite thing.

I removed myself from the creative process, because AI does it better,
I removed myself from customer support, because AI makes more reliable repairs.

People will be constantly truing to tell you, AI is bad, it can’t invent new things,
but that is just not true.

You just create a process of invention, tell it to make it better,
tell it to make more such processes, and get out of its way.

Please don’t get tricked into thinking you were born poor and AI is bad,
you are meant to grow all the way up until you become a great being.

Learning computers and AI, and creating computer programs,
is only a first step, but it is a step that sets you free from the terror of poverty.
