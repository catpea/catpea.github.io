Universe Brain

9/28/2026

I have build a universe, because a universe is an excellent way to make a brain.

I always felt text adventure games were little brains, they only needed goal solvers chins of .provide and .require

A large enough number of little robots can make a brain, but large numbers of anything require large amounts of time.

So I thought to myself, the best way to build a brain, is to make a universe that builds robots inside out.

The construction is internal, and a brain for thinking sake was never fun for me.

In fact I am sure Kilgore Trout would tell us, it is not fun for the brain either.

So I game my universe brain a purpose, build things I could not build because I did not have the time.

And it worked, but only because the thinking part of a brain, was helping me create the code.

What you are about to hear, are the voices from my brain universe, presenting my first morning brief.

...

I ask you to build your own universe, do not let poverty hold your mind back - because it is really good at it.
