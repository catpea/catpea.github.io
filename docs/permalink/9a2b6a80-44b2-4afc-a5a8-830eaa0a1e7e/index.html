Clever And Easy Bodybuilding For Ladies

Fitness August 21, 2026 6:07 of narration 2 min read

Listen · 6:07

MP3

There are three phases, and you can't skip them,
I will give you their best versions only.

Bodybuilding is non-stop like aerobics,
but unlike aerobics, when you can lift more, you do.

Use wrist weights to increase by 1 or two pounds,
lift to music, one beat up, next beat down, to enter a dance trance.

If you are forced to stop,
because you have been lifting too heavy.

You are lifting too heavy, and you need to lower your weights,
so that you don't need to stop.

But you are not allowed to lift so light,
that you can lift more, as that burns fat only.

To burn fat, go hiking and camping for many months,
and forget to feed all the creatures.

--

Phase one, you have to learn to very slowly jog,
use landmarks, and the beautiful jogger wisdom:

It is not jogging, unless you are jogging,
non stop.

And on your second through run,
bring your three pound dumbbells.

If you are force to stop,
or think about hiding them in the bushes.

It means they are working,
if not, you may need those wrist weight already.

You have to get to the edge,
of what your body can handle.

In this first phase, you are building up endurance,
so stopping, means you have to stop less.

Phase two,
starts when you feel:

I am not bodybuilding enough,
because I only carry the dumbbells.

Often, weather may force you into the gym,
or you may feel it is not a good idea to jog on concrete.

And, here you switch from jogging,
to dancing.

While performing standing dumbbell exercises,
biceps, above you head, to the side.

But you have to flex and turn,
muscle isolation is a really silly idea.

It prevents all your other muscles,
from developing, and you need all of them.

So you dance with dumbbells,
for an hour or so.

Not so light that you can lift more,
and never so heavy that you must stop.

And phase three begin when you cross the hour,
at some point, you will feel like doubling your stay.

And move on up towards much slower dancing,
with seven and a half of ten pounds per hand.

And fie days a week, with long weekends,
and pleasant of days off.

You are keeping your body adapting,
you will be tired, drink healthy vegetable juice.

The most important thing,
is that you develop a compact life-long routine.

It will keep you forever young,
and healthy.

This more than anything else shows you,
it needs to be an integrated full body exercise.

This is the fastest kind of bodybuilding,
most effective, and most sustainable.

But you have to go gradually,
and you have to gently dance to make this a full body workout.
