Artificial Intelligence Power!

Artificial Intelligence April 4, 2026 7:05 of narration 3 min read

Listen · 7:05

MP3

Well it is now possible to create programs without much programming,
and begin building up a battle against poverty.

AI changes the way programs are written, they used to require knowhow,
today you speak to AI in user interface patters, like concept map or timeline.

And stich your ideas together out of other applications,
old, new, weird, boring, anything that is interesting to you.

It is that personal interest that will drive the project, and set you apart.
so pick what you like, what moves you.

You don’t have to finish your projects, because it is not about completing,
but constantly learning and practicing.

In the end it won’t just be the code you create, but how you can bend it,
do navigate around obstacles or do unforeseen things.

It is the practice of a hundred unfinished projects,
that will help you the most.

Old programmers know nothing, they don’t even understand,
that each region of your program needs an AI, that specializes in that spot.

They can’t comprehend, how the job of this strange little AI,
is to give your users the ability to enrich that area, whatever it is.

It can be expansion of an action or command library, that makes your program,
truly belong to your customer.

It can be a builder of more AI,
or a builder of entire new panes or windows in the application.

Programmers are too busy to consider you expanding the reach of your apps,
into browsers via local-add-ons.

They don’t understand you can create applications that local ai crawls,
on the surface, building that out automatically.

To this day, 40 months into AI, few programmers fully grasp,
what autonomous AI means.

This is your world, it does not begin with code anymore,
it begins with shipping features, that would shatter a programmers mind.

They could never dream about the scale of features a one person can create,
they were always so desperate to hand onto control as not to get lost.

Now, you are a giant,
even if you are new to programming.

Ignore the critics, the real programmers fell silent last year,
they don’t have time to speak - they have infinite power.

Don’t just create applications for your users,
create applications that build applications.

Don’t just use AI, put it in a world you create, and strengthen it,
have it argue with it self, refining its creations, with multiple personas.

Your biggest fear should be thinking small,
it will take you a long while before you climb above your won ideas to see big.

Think of an application you like,
and tell your AI that you want to build a web version, using modern JavaScript.

Break it up into components you can preview and test in isolation
and embed an in-browser AI into it, to eliminate grunt work.

Please learn to dream big,
so that your local AI is hard at work programming especially while you sleep.

Authentication, reverse proxy, network services, containers, firewall,
and may you never lose a singe cycle.
