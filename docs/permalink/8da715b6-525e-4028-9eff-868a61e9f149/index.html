The Great American CEO: Young Ladies

Artificial Intelligence September 3, 2026 4:32 of narration 2 min read

Listen · 4:32

MP3

As you can imagine, each time a new age comes,
the parasites try to hang on for dear life.

Artificial intelligence rung in a new age,
and parasites work hard to keep it quiet.

Your High School education will be misleading,
and it will be branded with some company.

Resist, there is no such thing as free computers,
you are not a resource to be traded.

AI will never be good at open ends,
that is something you are uniquely good at.

You are now in the business,
of creating unicorns.

Unique, powerful, disruptive, sticky,
resilient companies that slay competition.

That remind venture capitol to hold its tongue,
as you are the leader and they a bottom feeder.

Others will create a store,
that customers have to pay monthly for.

You create a free store,
that comes with unique AI generated products that can already be sold.

You create self provisioning networks,
tools to generate more products, that move the user,

And extra plugins for professionals,
few of which, will make you money.

Everything here and beyond,
is AI built, which is what makes you the CEO.

You have to route the AI,
away from getting confused.

Remind it what the new mission is,
and route against being strictly responsible.

Choose savant,
over bureaucratic perfectionist.

You have to use desktop applications,
to eliminate the need for servers.

Use AI to make your software,
infinitely powerful.

Ask AI for self provisioning, self recovering,
non-uniform replicated service meshes that can't crash.

Use the power of text adventure games,
over the primitive and constrained command line.

Don't starve the world, make it bloom, containerize it,
test it with AI inside out, and control it.

Each new invention,
changes the world.

If you fall behind,
the era before will hold you back.

Once,
the personal computer was unlike anything ever before.

Today, it is the Thinking Machine,
the doing machine, the never tiring machine.

Begin building your AI automated companies immediately,
so that you have the time to fail a thousand times.
