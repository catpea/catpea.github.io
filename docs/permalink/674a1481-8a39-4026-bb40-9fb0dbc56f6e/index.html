Loving Fitness Advice For Ballooning Women

Fitness October 25, 2026 5:41 of narration 2 min read

Listen · 5:41

MP3

I fixed my knees with exercise, with dancing at the gym,
despite the funny title, this is the most serious advice you will ever receive.

You, personally, have been granted the miracle of life,
you will protect it, you first, your heath first.

No more compromise, in any compromise between good an evil,
only evil can profit.

Our teenage days give us a fitness momentum,
the firecrackers we once were gave us our muscle and health.

As we get older that momentum fades,
as we move less it dispersal faster.

And our teenage momentum runs out,
we start getting deformed.

Let us begin at the start,
you were never poor.

There is a political carousel that hoist stupid men with money,
so some measure of impression of power.

Those men believe themselves superior to you,
because you are a worker.

The most unhinged, evil of them,
call you a breeder.

They make poverty a reality,
because you think it is just something that happened to you.

The fathers of your children, are often just boys,
men, or gentlemen are rare, and never make themselves visible as such.

You are not a servant, you are warrior,
your babies will prefer to see you rise.

This is really all the fitness advice you need,
once you get rid of your problems, you get rid of your ailments.

You will get up, and go shopping for a backpack, three season tent,
and a new life, on the Appalachian Trail.

They will tell legends about you,
and your trail name, will become synonymous with Grace and Trail Magic.

More children need your help,
than those that call you mother.

Your body needs timer o repair it self,
for a city slicker obesity is death.

But, on the trail, it is energy, warmth,
muscle, the perfect food for recovery, rejuvenation, and rage.

Never quiet,
rage.

Whether your life was good, happy, both or neither,
you are no longer an ordinary person.

Use narrated books to learn about adventurers and adventure,
take time to inherit the culture of their greatness and stitch your own.

The Appalachian Trail, The Pacific Crest Trail, and The Continental Divide,
have wept your trail name in their sacred beauty – you have been called.
