Fake Education Is A Dead End

Education May 4, 2026 5:50 of narration 3 min read

Listen · 5:50

MP3

Do not attempt to test if the world is ran,
by people who peaked in High School.

You have been artificially dragged down,
by lies, poverty, fake education, and walls everywhere.

It is so bad that you think of people who reject that trash,
as gifted.

But you are just as gifted,
so as long as you start learning on your own.

Maybe, ages ago, you had an argument against programming,
but now with AI typing all the code, you are free to invent.

So your first act of rebellion is learning how to program,
with the help of AI.

And then you setup an automated business that takes care of it self,
it is like building a walking robot, but in the business world.

This is about building virtual worlds with objects,
so that you can use them as symbolic workflow engines.

But the point is to get out, and grab onto something powerful,
programming with being one of the most amazing things.

And not to participate in fake education, fake it until you make it,
or pretending to learn.

You have an infinite mind, being kept stressed out,
just as soon as "vacation is over" became depressing.

You needed to start learning on your own,
that feeling of doom is a real wisdom.

It is possible to learn programming in school,
but it will be hundreds of times slower.

And you will learn the wrong languages,
and in a wrong sequence on top of that.

They'll get you every which way,
and still dance on your grave singing "Balanced Education".

Every scientist, every philosopher, every authentic leader,
learned on their own.

You may learn from others,
but only when your own efforts outline a gap you can inherit into.

You have to have the need for it,
and that again takes you back to learning on your own.

Do not ask, if following everybody else's directions,
will destroy your life, despite their good intentions.

Just start learning what move you today,
move onto what moves you next week.

And wait until you eventually return to the firs thing,
that will be your circle of curiosity.

On your second pass, you begin rising,
above that you have already learned.

Stress is everywhere, only hiking and camping, for weeks at a time,
until you are ready for narrated books.

And then frequently returning to the trails for a few years,
can set you free.

If you try to learn programming tomorrow, again they got you,
stress will trick you into thinking you are not smart enough.

You don't just need a vacation,
vacation is how you live, and learn, and thrive, and grow.

What they've been calling vacation, is actually the normal life,
they people who see you as a resource to be mined use shame to box you in.

Don't let them make you feel guilty for living, get out of the fog,
you are meant to grow all the way up until you become a great being.
