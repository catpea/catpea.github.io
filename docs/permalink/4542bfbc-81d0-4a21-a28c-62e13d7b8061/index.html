Aipocalypse Meow: The Prices Went Up, And Here Is What You Need

Artificial Intelligence April 28, 2026 4:21 of narration 2 min read

Listen · 4:21

MP3

Aipocalypse, you will discover, works for you,
and against the profiteering gluttons.

Who are only concerned,
with selling you, what you are actually willing to pay for.

Start by asking your AI what the best Open Source AI Agent is,
I ended up using "Hermes Agent" with a Hermes recommended AI model.

We live in the age of AI, and you will have to use AI,
to teach you how to install your own Agent, and a local Model.

There is no escaping this, you need local AI,
with a powerful creative well pampered open source agent.

The agent is a harness around AI or LLM that uses clever tricks,
to make it smarter, more knowledgeable, and give it a stronger mind.

The whole point of artificial intelligence,
is that you don't have to pay for it, is that you wave your own.

Is it as smart as the expensive ones for programming,
as you have heard, programming has changed.

And when you upgrade your architectural ideas, your skills,
it will be just as powerful as the state of the art frontier models.

Imagine how operating systems work, they have a lot of commands,
that you put together to do things you need.

And that is also what works well for AI,
you make little commands.

Where each command does one thing well for your application needs,
and then you move higher on the conceptual level.

And you start using those commands to make programs,
this way your AI does not need to know about the code in commands.

And you don't need the big memory,
at this point you introduce procedures and skills.

You may even consider using symbolic AI,
which is just dependency resolution.

A symbolic action aside from payload has a provides and requires,
and a planner can titch it all together to meet your goal state.

We are currently programming wrong,
and that is why AI needs so much context or memory.

Once we switch to Command or Acton based programming,
it will all be just little programs.

The powerful AI, also makes mistakes,
dumping a lot of code into context space is not without problems.

In summary, it got expensive, you have to bring AI home,
and it will be smart, because we have to change the way we program.
