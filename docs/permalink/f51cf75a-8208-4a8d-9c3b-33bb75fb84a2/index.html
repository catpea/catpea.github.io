Finishing Your Software, For Young Ladies

Programming October 31, 2026 10:57 of narration 3 min read

Listen · 10:57

MP3

This is easy, you just need three ideas, that you are already familiar with,
Desktop, Market, and Workbench.

To truly finish your application, and make it make sense to multiple users,
on a server accessible from the internet, potentially selling it and memberships even.

You need to use Linux user accounts,
similar to how you have multiple accounts on your devices.

And upon subscription and login, your user needs to be shown a Web Desktop,
with the first icon being Marker, and app store where your apps can be installed from.

This way your programming AI has a clear mission, and none of your users,
will be confused, they already know how login, desktop and app store works.

You can of course pre-install applications,
that make sense for whatever business you are running.

And then there is the workbench, developing applications for your desktop,
has to happen within that desktop, convenience, clarity, simplicity.

Before we begin let me warn you, all your computer programs,
need an internal model, an abstract machine that comes first.

When that machine or model changes, your UI updates,
creating applications without an internal model may confuse AI.

Everybody thinks they know what that model is, but AI needs to operate it,
and some of your applications may need to work in VR using WebXR.

And I believe the model, the machine, the application heart,
is described by you and shaped by AI in a 3D workbench.

Discovering internal applications models that work for you,
will be a pleasant journey, because the model must be simple to understand.

Be aware of the model,
with that said let us continue.

But to mange the model, and have your AI program everything as you monitor,
it needs to be a 3D application, because you are making a lot of stuff.

UI components are listening to the model,
buttons send actions that change the model.

You may have a tree of objects like world, country, state, city,
or maybe tiny database tables.

Where for example one user id exists twice in email table,
meaning that they have two emails in their private profiles, or many something.

Or spreadsheets where a formula declares dependency on cells,
and monitors all cells for change to update its output and emit change.

Maybe a tiny virtual file system, easy to inspect with a file manager,
and it makes managing files of a project painless.

To see all the connections you need some kind of a futuristic user interface,
plus, you need to start thinking about developing 3D applicaions.

AI is great at creating 3D software, you may ask it to create a futuristic UI,
and then have good success with it reproducing that image with real 3D graphics.

And finally, when you click Build or Publish,
the application you have been working on.

Simply appears in the Market or App Store.
that all of your users already have access to.

You see what I am trying to show you, Desktop, Market, Workbench,
all combine to create a system that lets you finish.

Without Desktop, Market, Workbench, even if you have a complete app,
there are questions.

Where do you put it, how do you privately save files for multiple users,
how do you make sure security is strong.

And even something as simple as: can the user download all their files?
Maybe they are closing their account, maybe it is required by law where they live.

All of these questions,
are very strongly answered.

You put it on a linux server you rent for less than 20 dollars,
sometimes a lot less, you just ask your AI where you can get at linux.

You privately and securley,
save files in normal Linux user accounts.

And when the user wants their files,
you just zip up their home directory and let them download it.

In the age of AI, your AI will take care of everything here,
it just needs to know what you intended.

Copy and paste this text,
and let it take it from there.
