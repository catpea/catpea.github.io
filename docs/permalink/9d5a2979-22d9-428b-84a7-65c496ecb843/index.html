Systems Control For Young Ladies

Artificial Intelligence November 17, 2026 5:27 of narration 2 min read

Listen · 5:27

MP3

I created Pea OS to see if the desktop metaphor,
can successfully help with controlling complex systems.

And it reveals that many different apps are required,
an app is a view of a thing.

And to give you a proper example of a complex system,
imagine your first on-line business.

You need a desktop that isn't your computers desktop,
and you need those icons always in precise locations.

And you are going to arrange things your way,
and if anybody touches your icons they die.

And the icons would have AI made apps,
that abstract away domain registration and server hosting.

Think of them as one time apps, just for your business,
and they only have what you need, and no more.

And it is not just apps,
where AI simplifies domain name registration.

Feedback apps, monitoring apps,
security apps live here.

And again I want to underline,
these are not generic applications.

AI makes them for you,
to match your business needs.

It is not a perfect solution,
and my experimentation continues.

But, I am certain, that there cannot exist,
just one graphical representation of a complex system.

You need multiple views, multiple apps,
all in one place that capture just what you need.

And it is just as important,
that AI can use it too.

It is important to notice,
how the desktop is for humans first and AI second.

We have to admit defeat, somewhat,
because AI can't know your vision.

So you, and not AI,
is put in charge of arranging the icons.

And stating what apps you need,
and what they must look like.

Pea OS includes multiple desktops,
and that takes views to a higher level.

In a serious version of a proper control desktop,
You would have a billing desktop, management desktop.

Monitoring desktop, not only would you have different views,
you would have multiple groups of views.

And to the naysayers, it is not possible to use 30 websites,
and hope to get stuff done, it takes forever.

I don't want to over-complicate this,
but the desktop is narrows what AI does to control things for you.

It also uses the tools on the desktop,
now through internal window control.

But its vision is not perfectly clear,
Its management tasks are on your management desktop.

Unbelievably, I am against,
connecting the apps together with pipes or wires.

Give the AI a procedure a SKILL that you first test,
and let it move between apps on its own.

The desktop metaphor, is far superior to dashboards,
and is likely yet to be fully explored.

For one, use your scroll wheel,
to pan all windows all at once in Pea OS.
