Fancy Business; Or, Artificial Intelligence Can Help You Tower

Artificial Intelligence October 8, 2026 6:33 of narration 3 min read

Listen · 6:33

MP3

Procedures, including procedures for discovery and invention are the key,
and you should focus on software that helps people get stuff done.

Now more then ever, everything is moving at a faster pace,
people don’t have time to spin up a separate department to do side jobs.

All the resources are focused on the main goal, and they can’t redirect them,
but they will buy what they need, because it instantly improves their aim.

Making a strategic purchase, will benefit them more than you can imagine,
and they will only spend money, instead of their employees or AI credits.

The interesting part happens when you try to gag them,
with the sheer beauty and completeness and coverage of your product.

They’ll look at your website, and say, wow I wish I thought of that,
they will see instantly how you make money and how it is a good idea.

Of course you have to like working on your product,
because it is not just about taking that extra step.

With the power of Artificial Intelligence you stick your head out,
look at the world of your business, and for a moment tower over competition.

In my undertaking, I am going up against hateful, and cranky, gatekeepers,
thankfully I am not the first, but I am the best.

Which will make the people who came before me, also angry,
designers pride themselves in designing and pixel perfect rendering.

Always be ready with a backup plan, because when you climb to the top,
people will be attacking your company with down-votes and false claims.

You may have your main email suspended by your competitors,
and you certainly should not expect money people being kind to you.

I made that mistake when not even selling anything, and got banned,
because I didn’t have enough software that used their system.

Don’t go out there with an open heart, you put on your armor, get your sword,
and approach the world of business with an army of 10,000 ai employees.

When your product is something you like, you’ll know who to talk to,
when it is beautiful you will know where to show it, and not fear critics.

It is very important to practice speaking with your AI, for example,
when it comes to complicated things, I never directly speak to the agent.

I first ask a general purpose AI to refine what I am hoping to say,
and send that version, it is literally the version you hoped you could write.

Finally, if you are just beginning, use all of AI,
image generation, video generation, narration, music, characters.

Use AI for operating system preparation and maintenance,
get it to give you local AI that you can experiment with.

Use every scrap of its ability, don’t assume you don’t need it,
prove it to yourself that you won’t be needing it, after-all.

In my product, I use lower quality but beautiful images for sketching,
for concept art, it made total sense to regenerate the gibberish or blur I liked.

But it turned out, that I needed to explode the objects into separate pieces,
and then regenerate what was behind them, it greatly improved my art.

Push, as experiment, push as a way to move forward,
and push, on principle, just to see what hapens.

And the way you can visualize this in your diagrams is achievements,
at every big step think to yourself you unlocked a new achievement.

For a moment until your competition catches up,
or you get bogged down with well paying clients, you will be the best.

That is the reason why AI is not welcome everywhere,
because it give you the power of titans, the power to rise, and for a time tower.
