Autonomous Artificial Intelligence For Young Ladies

10/22/2026

Heads up! you must always hand the AI its keyboard back, an AI generated program must also become an autonomous AI managed company.

You tell it to teake care of product advertising and earning to pay its own bills, please, at no point should you step in with manual labor - that is not how it is done.

When AI tells you it has finished your program, you have to ask it to verify and complete what needs work.

You are not done asking AI for work when it produces your program, now you have to ask AI to finish, publish, and check if everything works.

There is no such thing as a finished program, that is when you use AI to take care of everything else.

And you have to tell it to do so autonomously, without asking you, though you use an AI to say that, human language is too flimsy.

AI may tell you that, project analysis requires human input or validation, but you turn that around and say you have to study and test your knowledge.

And improve the program, and refine the process, and safely take care of every detail.

One of my programs has an incomplete menu, and it is a terrible idea to tell the AI to finish it.

You have to tell the AI to build up the skills needed to ensure it is fully usable, to refine those feature, and make the program user friendly.

And then use your incomplete menu, as a measuring stick.

The skills that the AI builds up belong in the program, and in the future you can use an agent to scan for useful skills.

When it says that it needs verification, you find a rich way that benefits the program and reply with that.

Basically, stating “No, you do it”, and eventually you just have to talk to smarter AI to get it to do it.

In closing, please understand that your program idea maybe beautiful, but only AI can make the tree bloom with advanced features.

You can’t hope to publish the website, take care of the billing, setup the downloads, you have to make this process autonomous.

You must not provide support, though you should read emails, but AI is the only powerful tool that can do the bug fixes.

You are not waiting for the AI to finish, you must always turn the keyboard back to AI, and say you do it.

That is the meaning of the words artificial intelligence, you let it do the product thinking so that you can manage several companies.

But please, do not communicate with AI working on a complex task directly, if one does break out of its autonomous loop.

Keep a chat about this behavior with another AI, and tell it that you are being asked questions that AI must handle on its own.

And your chat AI, will have the mindset to tell your complex worker AI, to responsible return to the autonomous way.

Finally, the building up of SKILL files means you can use local AI, once the SKILL is written in stone local AI can handle it.

To test this you t ask it to quiz it self, and where it failed, teach it self, until it is able to pass the test and can carry on.

Ultimately when something goes wrong, you instruct it to learn from smarter AI.

Thus, removing yourself, from the process entirely.

Do you understand, when it comes to powerful software, your job is to step away and let the thinking machines make things even better.

You etch the pathways, configure the company, and step away, let it run it self, and pay its own bills, using its powerful intelligence.
