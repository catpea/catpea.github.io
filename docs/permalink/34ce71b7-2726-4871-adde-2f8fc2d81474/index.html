A Letter To Joe Six Pack: Restart Your Quest At Chattahoochee National Forest

Society October 14, 2026 7:42 of narration 3 min read

Listen · 7:42

MP3

Let us define our terms first, Joe Six Pack, is a derogatory term,
that you must never use, that your false leaders use to mark you as a slave.

You will never find happiness, because it was already taken from you,
your genius was trashed by politicians making you poor on purpose.

The consequences of your actions, that you wish you didn’t take,
they were arranged my movies, TV shows, commercials, and fake educucation.

You were never a shitty student, school was already using you as a resource,
you’ve been strip mined ever since you were a child.

In US much luckier than in less developed countries,
there you are not just a slave, worker drone, and workforce, but a soldier.

Some nations, not only take genius, but drive a dog sickness into you,
fear of feeding former pets, or dog eat dog, or more likely dog shit dog.

The dog shit dog is a culture of prevention others from rising,
so that you don’t have to rise yourself.

You were never a C student,
the Cs came from your teachers looking at grades in other subjects.

That is why you always had shit grades, you sere sold out,
by everyone, you will be sold out by your foreman, boss, and leaders.

There are no political parties, both ends are the same,
they are there to use you.

The only hope your children had, was you seeing the lies,
and running for office, and dodging well.

You were the ope and dream of the future generations,
you were supposed to grow all the way up and become a great being.

That is why they made you a slave,
because the parasites only profit from the compromise between good and evil.

And in any compromise between the two, only evil profits,
nobody but the good profit from your decency, class, dignity, and wisdom.

You have been defeated, but something unpredictable happened,
dreamers found a way to copy thinking, into machines.

And now robots with hands and fingers are smarter than you,
more capable than you, and cheaper than you.

Your slavers, assumed you would be the slave, the asset the resource,
but they found something cheaper and better – you are about to get fired.

Take this opportunity,
to open your eyes.

They will spin anti-science narratives,
that resonate with the mind they hollowed out, to drain you of what is left.

But you are being asked to rise, if they did not yet take your health,
hurry, walk the Appalachian Trail from Springer Mountain to Mount Katahdin.

Do it now,
get up and drive to Georgia.

For the rest of you, ready yourselves for the final battle,
the AI was not made for your slavers, it is here for all of us.

Use artificial intelligence to practice becoming an operator,
the AI does science you do control.

Build fake news, build computer games, build the high school,
that won’t betray your children the way you were betrayed.

The job you are about to lose, was not worthy of you,
the job of a Thinking Machine Operator, is worthy of you, it is you.

Take your knowledge, your experience, your craft,
and rise against your owners.

Let them choke on the fucking robots,
as you work to take over their manual labor industries.

Everything will hurt, now one will stand up for you,
you will lose everything, as you rise on your feet this time.

With a sword held up high,
the posture of the great being you were always meant to be.

Resist the propaganda,
build your wisdom starting at the center of your being.

This is your final battle,
they took your time and life and health.

But you will give the future generations,
freedom, wisdom, and your rage.

No more work, Springer Mountain, Georgia,
start walking north, until you reach Mount Katahdin in Maine.

This was your original Quest,
reclaim it.
