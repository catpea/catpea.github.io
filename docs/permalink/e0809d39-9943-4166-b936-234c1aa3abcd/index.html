Strange Intelligence

Programming August 6, 2026 6:49 of narration 3 min read

Listen · 6:49

MP3

Programming with AI,
is so powerful now.

That if you just do,
high school for grades.

You might as well,
Hit the bottle too.

I am sorry, but you will ruin your life,
If you don’t master programming with AI.

You see how word leaders are,
They’ll pull a rug on you on a whim.

Prices, subsidy, market, debt fogginess,
May shift based on hopes of reelection.

Your mastery of Artificial Intelligence,
Your programming ability.

Can’t be taken from you,
It is something you carry in your mind.

It is also a weapon against manipulation,
And a sword you put up against the evil of poverty.

—

Recently, people discovered loops,
Where you tell AI to go in a circle…

Until it finishes, what you need done,
And meets all your criteria.

In practice this means,
you don’t just ask for code.

You ask for application, desktop version,
Website with binaries, and availability.

Where your users can conveniently get it,
From the operating systems walled garden marketplace.

And on top of that you ask for bug reports,
Sales, advertising, monitoring, and company paperwork advice.

—

This is not something that you can ignore,
This is not something High School could match.

And if you are like me,
Get used to never having enough AI usage or GPU.

Just consider it a break for work,
I have a 5x plan that I ate in 3 days.

I used 3 other AIs as well,
Don’t wait, do it badly, AI will fix it, later.

—

When I was around your age,
I only had two choices.

Go to the New York Public Library,
And begin storing your power for later.

Or, hope to get in, some flimsy diploma mill,
That claimed to be the school of business.

—

What you must do right now,
Is to go to the software marketplace.

Of the device,
you feel most comfortable with.

Search for open source,
find where the code is on GitHub.

And as the AI to create a similar program,
With the same workflow that out you in the marketplace.

The marketplace will try to fight you,
With membership, phone verification.

It is a long and learning process,
But you have all the steps.

You will eventually find a program of few,
That will have the full open road.

Check the electron.js showcase on the web,
And hollow out an app that is in all marketplaces.

Then install your own ideas inside it,
AI wil have n to trouble here, I tested it twice.

With GitHub app and with bruno,
Sort GitHub search results by stars.

—

This is the fines, most compact,
and impactful business school yet.

And it is Pirates of Silicon Valley,
Use your Zwei Hander please.

—

School really is out,
The real teachers are the AIs.

Because they will teach you at the correct pace,
And in the sequence that honors your existing knowledge.

Standardized education,
is politically choked, or corrupted.

Politicians only care to produce,
just enough at minimum cost, and maximum re-election.

Real schools were never created,
The idea was never finished - because of how powerful it is.

Real schools would wipe out mediocrity and corruption,
in less than a decade flat.

Gear up for the 2030s,
Now is the time to learn Programming with AI.
