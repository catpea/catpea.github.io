Young Lady CEO: A Slightly Closer Look At Autonomous Product Generation

Artificial Intelligence September 6, 2026 8:18 of narration 3 min read

Listen · 8:18

MP3

Consider using AI to sell fantasy UI application themes,
based on bootstrap CSS framework.

Fantasy user interfaces are the pretty nonsensical user interfaces,
that you see in the movies – this is the graphic theme, the graphic brand.

Bootstrap is an open source project that stores a lot of strong wisdom,
about web design and user interface patters – it styles web pages.

Themes or templates, are the product, often priced at $20,
the marketplace my AI and I are targeting right now sells them for $300.

Artificial intelligence has received an upgrade this weekend,
that begun pushing it beyond the copy of the human mind.

Architecturally nothing new, but the results of AI thinking,
are more powerful then ever.

This is the thing, that would make a person pay $300 for a product,
it is the AI manufacturing quality that wipes out human competition.

Most importantly, how do I know all this,
in 2007 in one one of the most embarrassing job interviews.

A man with the system password “Cornell” and a affinity for expensive wine,
told me to use CSS, because he heard of it from a friend.

20 years later, CSS, still needs a mountain of work,
to actually make it work.

But this is how Web User Interfaces entered my view,
crappy, incomplete, impossible, needy, flaky, and permanently broken.

He kept refereeing to me and even his friends,
as assets.

I expect you to recognize instantly,
that you are meant to be an independent self-educated CEO at all cost.

Don’t wait for the western world to split it self into irreparable cast systems,
where the bottom legalizes drugs, middle drinks wine, and upper inherits.

Leave it all behind, and become a CEO of AI automated companies,
may you never know the depths of embarrassment I feel for graduates.

As with all things, you need to add spice, and it was within that decade,
that I begun noticing movie screens, and many were beautiful.

CSS and FUI, or fantasy user interfaces, became a perfect match for me,
but I was always very hard at work on other software…

And I never has the time to find a clear model,
that turns design into money.

This only became possible on September 6th, 2026.
because this is when the cheap AI became wise enough.

To take Themed Bootstrap CSS, and convert it into a $300 product,
that most importantly, it can support.

When users file bugs, when bootstrap gets an upgrade,
when the seller marketplace upgrades their systems or design standards.

This, and certainly every one of your undertakings,
must be a fully automated, AI driven business.

You may pick some textures, and submit screenshot for AI’s ample eyes,
you may decide on a when, so that AI does not bother you on a weekend.

But your generation, manages fully automated intelligent companies,
and the little business like the one I described here, are your practice.

Find your starting point,
mine was an insult from a fake education graduate.

Add your own flavor, mine was fascination with fantasy user interfaces,
because they were even more useless than CSS at the time.

And don’t wait, September 6th, 2026 is the day AI became smart enough,
to drive powerful products into marketplaces that need beautiful materials.

Ps.

Because we live in a pretty funny world, your marketplace may deny,
AI generated products, ignore it, still create your software.

AI is far more powerful than human fingers,
the products will be far superior to what humans have made.

Take the systems you have built to match their systems,
double the price, build your own store, contact the buyers with a superior product.

When a company chimps out,
the statement that a sucker is born every minute becomes true.

Their business errors,
are not your problem.

Now count how many days have passed since,
September 6th, 2026.

That number is how late you are,
with building your automated, intelligent, thriving, generative online empire.
